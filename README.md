# wro

> 日本語のREADMEはこちらです: [README.ja.md](README.ja.md)

A repository containing data on the World Robot Olympiad (WRO) Future Innovators competition.

## Data

This repository provides the evaluation criteria for the WRO Future Innovators competition, detailing the scoring weights for Elementary, Junior, and Senior age categories.

- [`future-innovators-criteria.csv`](future-innovators-criteria.csv): Scoring criteria in Japanese.

### Evaluation Criteria Preview

The table below is an English summary of the criteria and scoring points from the CSV file.

| Evaluation Area | Category | Elementary (8-12) | Junior (11-15) | Senior (14-19) |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| Idea, Quality, & Creativity | Project and Innovation | 30 | 30 | 20 |
| Research & Report | Project and Innovation | 15 | 15 | 15 |
| Social Impact & Needs | Project and Innovation | 15 | 10 | 10 |
| Key Innovation & Slogan | Project and Innovation | 10 | 10 | 10 |
| Entrepreneurship Elements | Project and Innovation | - | 10 | 10 |
| Next-Stage Prototype Development | Project and Innovation | - | - | 10 |
